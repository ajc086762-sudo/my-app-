Rules in this prototype
  • You control You (Player 1); three AI opponents take turns automatically.
  • Each player starts with $1,500. Passing Start pays $200; landing directly on Start pays $300.
  • If you decline an unowned property, an auction begins. The AI may also bid.
  • Property rent increases with upgrades. Owning a complete group adds a 50% rent bonus.
  • Manage upgrades during your turn; levels are limited to three and upgrades must be distributed evenly.
  • City Tax costs $100. Chance and Community Chest cards now have effects, including movement, payments, rewards and a Get Out of Jail Free card.
  • Jail: pay $100 to leave, use a Get Out of Jail Free card, or try for doubles up to three attempts. On the third failed attempt, pay $100 and move using that roll. Jail doubles do not grant an extra roll.
  • Property management: upgrade complete groups evenly, sell upgrades for 50% of their cost, mortgage eligible properties for 75% of price (upgrades stay attached), and repay mortgages at the original mortgage amount plus 10%.
  • Negotiated trades: buy properties from computer opponents or offer your properties to them for an agreed cash price. Properties are never sold directly back to the bank.
  • Payments use cash first, then sell upgrades, then mortgage eligible properties. If the player still cannot pay, they are bankrupt, properties are auctioned, and they are eliminated.
  • Victory: immediately reach $10,000 net worth, be the last player remaining, or have the highest net worth after 25 rounds. Ties use cash, then net worth excluding cash; a remaining tie is shared.
